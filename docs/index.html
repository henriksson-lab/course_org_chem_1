Organic Chemistry 1

A five-week course (5KE231, 7.5 credits, Umeå University) on how the structure of a molecule decides what it does: drawing structures, following electrons through reactions, and using spectra and lab data as evidence. R is used beside the lab notebook for calculations, plots and reports.

Before you start

  • Set up R and knit your first report, then download the student project.

Topics

Work through the sidebar from top to bottom. Topic days have a lecture, exercises, a self-check and an interactive demo; review days have mixed problems and a demo.

  • Week 1: Structures, properties, acids and bases. Read a skeletal structure, explain boiling points and solubility, and predict where a proton goes.
  • Week 2: Shape and electron flow. Conformations, stereochemistry and the curved-arrow language of mechanisms.
  • Week 3: Reactions at saturated carbon. Substitution, elimination, addition, and alcohol oxidation and reduction.
  • Week 4: Carbonyls and nitrogen. Addition to C=O, esters and amides, and amines and aromatic rings.
  • Week 5: Evidence. IR and NMR, synthesis and yield, and a final case that brings it all together.

See Canvas for dates, lab arrangements and assessment.
