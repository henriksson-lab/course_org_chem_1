Self-check: Alcohols and redox

Answer each question before opening its explanation. Draw the relevant structure or step when asked; recognition alone can hide a charge or geometry error.

1. What does propan-2-ol give on selected oxidation?

Retain the carbon skeleton.

Check your answer

Propanone. The carbon bearing OH becomes a carbonyl carbon, while its two carbon substituents remain attached.

2. Why is a tertiary alcohol a boundary case?

Explain the missing bond at the carbon bearing OH.

Check your answer

It has no C–H bond at that carbon. Simple conversion to C=O while retaining all three C–C bonds would overbond carbon. Harsh oxidation can involve C–C cleavage, outside this selected pattern.

3. What comes immediately after hydride-equivalent addition?

A ketone receives H at carbon while its pi pair moves to oxygen. Name the species type before work-up.

Check your answer

An alkoxide. A subsequent proton transfer from an explicit donor produces the alcohol. Free hydride in water is not what the simplified H: minus drawing represents.

4. Does an OH band prove which alcohol is present?

State a limitation of functional-group IR evidence.

Check your answer

No. Many compounds share that functional group, bands can overlap, and samples can contain water or mixtures. Combine multiple observations with structural and purity evidence.

5. Which amount limits a one-to-one reaction?

Reactants are available as 10.0 and 15.0 mmol. Product coefficient is one.

Check your answer

The 10.0 mmol reactant limits product to 10.0 mmol. Compare amount divided by stoichiometric coefficient, rather than raw masses.

6. Does 66.6% isolated yield mean 66.6% conversion?

Explain one possible reason they differ.

Check your answer

No. Starting material may react but product may be lost during isolation; side products or impurities can also affect the relationship. Conversion, isolated yield and purity answer different questions.

If an answer was unexpected, return to the worked practice, repair your own drawing and explain the change in a sentence.
